03 Case studies

Two workflows, end to end

A look at the operational bottlenecks businesses face, what we build to solve them, and the resulting impact on their bottom line.

Case study · SEO agency

Automated Technical SEO Auditor

Eliminating manual website audits for SEO agencies

3 days → <2 min per website audit

01 The bottleneck

An SEO agency was paying analysts to manually click through hundreds of client web pages to verify H1 tag presence, category optimization, and Product/FAQ schema markup. It was burning valuable billable hours on repetitive data verification.

02 What we built

A bulk auditing pipeline built in Python. The agency drops a list of URLs into the system, and the scraper automatically crawls the pages, parses the HTML and JSON-LD, and generates a clean report highlighting exactly which pages are missing critical schemas or tags.

03 The ROI

Website audits that previously took three days are now completed in under two minutes. By driving the time-cost of an audit to near-zero, the agency now uses these automated reports as instant, free lead magnets to close new clients.

Case study · Content marketing

YouTube Audience Intelligence

Automating content strategy and market research

−90% market research time

01 The bottleneck

A content marketing team struggled to find data-backed video topics. Their research process involved manually watching competitor videos, guessing keywords, and endlessly scrolling through YouTube comments to find what viewers were actually asking.

02 What we built

A custom data extraction and filtering engine. The team inputs keywords or channel URLs, and the system instantly extracts video metadata (filtered by length, language, and views) and scrapes the comments. An AI layer then identifies and groups actual viewer questions by topic.

03 The ROI

Market research time was reduced by 90%. Instead of guessing what to create, the agency now pitches lucrative content retainers based on the exact, unanswered questions their clients' target audiences are already asking on YouTube.
